Tres Leches Cake Recipe

Ingredients for Tres Leches Cake:

For the Cake Layers

1 cup all-purpose flour
1 and 1/2 tsp baking powder
1/4 tsp salt
5 large eggs, separated
1 cup sugar, divided into 3/4 and 1/4 cups
1 tsp vanilla extract
1/3 cup 2% or whole milk

For the Three-Milk Syrup

8 oz evaporated milk
9 oz sweetened condensed milk
1/4 cup heavy whipping cream

For the Whipped Cream and Decoration

2 1/2 cups heavy whipping cream
2 1/2 tablespoons granulated sugar
Fresh strawberries (about 1 lb)

Baking Steps

Prepare and Bake the Cake

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Butter and flour two 9-inch round cake pans.
  2. Set up three mixing bowls: one medium bowl and two large bowls. In the medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder and salt. Set aside.
  3. Separate the eggs, placing the yolks in one large bowl and the whites in the other. Beat the egg yolks with 3/4 cup of sugar on high speed for about 2 minutes, until pale yellow. Mix in the milk and vanilla extract.
  4. Add the flour mixture to the egg-yolk mixture. Beat on low speed just until combined.
  5. Using clean, dry beaters, beat the egg whites on high speed for about 1 minute, until soft peaks form. Gradually add the remaining 1/4 cup of sugar and beat until stiff peaks form, but do not let the whites become dry.
  6. Gently fold the egg whites into the yolk-and-flour mixture until just combined. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared cake pans.
  7. Bake for about 30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean. Turn the cakes onto a wire rack and let them cool to room temperature.
  8. When the cakes have cooled, use a serrated knife to trim their edges if needed.

Prepare the Three-Milk Syrup

  1. In a large measuring cup or bowl with a pouring spout, stir together the evaporated milk, sweetened condensed milk and 1/4 cup of heavy whipping cream.

Make the Whipped Cream

  1. For best results, chill a mixing bowl and the beaters or whisk attachment in the freezer for about 15 minutes. Make sure the heavy whipping cream is very cold.
  2. Beat the 2 1/2 cups of heavy whipping cream with the granulated sugar on high speed for about 1 1/2 to 2 minutes, until thick and spreadable. Do not overbeat. Refrigerate until needed.

Assemble the Cake

  1. Place one cake layer, flat side down, on a serving platter. Use a fork to poke holes all over the top, reaching close to the edges and through the depth of the cake. Spoon half of the three-milk syrup evenly over it. Allow the cake to absorb the syrup.
  2. Spread a generous layer of whipped cream over the first cake layer and arrange sliced strawberries on top.
  3. Place the second cake layer on top, with its flat side facing up. Poke holes all over its surface with a fork.
  4. Spoon the remaining syrup evenly over the second layer. Let it stand for 10 to 15 minutes so the syrup can soak in.
  5. Spread the remaining whipped cream over the top and sides of the cake. Decorate with fresh strawberries.
  6. Keep the finished cake refrigerated until it is ready to serve.
